2. What this notice covers

This website is TIFAND's Team Capability Portfolio. We use it to show clients and principal contractors the qualifications and experience of our team when we bid for work. It involves personal data about two groups of people:

  • TIFAND employees, whose CVs and certificates appear in the portfolio (section 3).
  • Visitors who view the website, such as tender evaluators (section 4).

3. Employees' data in the portfolio

What we publish

  • Name, post-nominal letters, job title and the date the employee joined TIFAND.
  • A professional profile, core skills, career history, education, professional memberships and languages, taken from the employee's CV.
  • Copies of degree certificates, professional registrations, training and health and safety certificates. These can include certificate or registration numbers and issuers' signatures.

We do not publish home addresses, personal phone numbers or email addresses, dates of birth, photo identity cards or any special category data such as health information. Certificates that showed a date of birth or a photo identity card have been removed from the website.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use this information to show clients that our people are qualified and competent for the work we bid for. Clients and principal contractors routinely require this evidence in tenders and for site competence checks.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): our interest in winning and delivering contracts, which also supports our employees' work. We have limited the information to what a client needs to assess competence. Employees can object to this use at any time (see section 9).

4. Visitors to this website

We do not ask visitors for their name, email address or organisation, and we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

  • Acceptance of the notice. When you accept the data protection and confidentiality notice, your browser remembers this in its session storage, so you are not asked again while the browser stays open. This stays on your own device, is not sent to us and is deleted when you close the browser.
  • Security check. Your answer to the security question is checked in your browser and is not sent to us or stored.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep its service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts and other resources. All fonts, images and documents are served from this website itself. No requests are made to other services such as Google Fonts.

5. Who can see the data

We share the portfolio link only with organisations evaluating TIFAND for a bid, tender or contract, and their evaluation teams. Before viewing, every visitor must accept our confidentiality and data protection terms. These limit use to that evaluation, prohibit copying or onward sharing without our written consent, and require deletion once the evaluation is complete.

GitHub, Inc. hosts the website on our behalf. We do not sell personal data or use it for marketing.

6. International transfers

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States, so hosting the website involves transferring data outside the UK. We rely on appropriate safeguards recognised under UK data protection law for these transfers, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

An employee's information stays in the portfolio while they work for TIFAND and the documents remain relevant. We remove an employee's information when they leave TIFAND, when a document is replaced, or when they ask us to remove it. We aim to do this within one month.

Visitors' session storage is cleared automatically when the browser is closed.

8. Security

The website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, is excluded from search engines and is shared only with the organisations described in section 5. Certificate copies have been reduced to what a client needs to verify competence. Access to the website files is limited to authorised TIFAND staff.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used
  • access your personal data
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict how your data is used
  • object to our use of your data, including our legitimate interests
  • data portability, where it applies

To use any of these rights, contact TIFAND Limited at the address or telephone number in section 1. We will respond without undue delay and within one month.
